# Privacy Policy

This is a self-hosted instance of Preloop. Privacy policies are determined by your organization's administrator.

For the open-source project privacy information, please visit [https://github.com/preloop/preloop](https://github.com/preloop/preloop).

What Preloop stores, what it sends, and how session content is used: see [Security & Privacy](https://github.com/preloop/preloop/blob/main/docs/security/security-privacy.md#your-data).
